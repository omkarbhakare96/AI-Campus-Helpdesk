How the AI pipeline works

Every complaint runs through five NLP models before a human ever sees it.

🏷️

Classification

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression sorts each complaint into one of 12 departments — from IT/Network to Security.

⚡

Priority prediction

Severity language in your text (hazards, outages, safety risks) is used to predict Low, Medium, High or Critical priority.

🙂

Sentiment analysis

Detects whether a complaint is written in frustration, neutrally, or as positive feedback.

🔎

Duplicate detection

Cosine similarity against every open ticket flags near-identical complaints before they clutter the queue.

🎯

Confidence score

Every ticket ships with an AI confidence score so admins know how much to trust the automated tags.

📊

Live tracking

Students track status by ticket ID; admins get a full dashboard with search, filters and analytics.
